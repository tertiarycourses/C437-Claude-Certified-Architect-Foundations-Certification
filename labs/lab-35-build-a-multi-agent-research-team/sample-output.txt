- **Creative industries (general)**: 78% of creative studios use generative AI every week (vendor opt-in poll of 640 customers of an AI image-tool seller, not peer-reviewed; measures weekly use rather than adoption, so unsupported as a general measure). (BrightCanvas-Customer-Benchmark-2026.pdf, p.3)
