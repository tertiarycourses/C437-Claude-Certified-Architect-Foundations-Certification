# AI Impact on the Healthcare Industry: Cited Evidence Report

Evidence on AI in healthcare is growing but largely vendor, secondary or undated. Radiology AI shows accuracy drops on external data, and scribe RCTs show modest burnout and workload gains. Early AI-discovered drugs report Phase I success but no approvals. Payer AI rules are active, with unverified claims of denial reductions. Funding and adoption are rising, but the measures differ. Patient-facing AI, liability, WHO guidance, nursing workforce and market forecasts were not covered.

- **clinical diagnostics**: A third-party tracker (unverified, not checked against FDA) reports about 1,614 FDA AI-enabled device authorizations through June 2026, about 1,230 radiology-led and 154 cardiovascular. (https://www.arca.inc/tools/ai-device-search)
- **clinical diagnostics**: A peer-reviewed systematic review reports radiology AI loses accuracy on external data, with the largest drops often in specificity (false-positive risk), though no figures are given in the synthesis. (https://doi.org/10.1097/MS9.0000000000004166)
- **clinical diagnostics**: A peer-reviewed narrative review (weaker evidence than a systematic review) reports vision-language models are less accurate on images from Black and female patients, with no figures given. (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s44398-025-00020-7)
- **clinical diagnostics**: A secondary review posted on ResearchGate (publication status unconfirmed) reports strong academic model accuracy alongside low clinical adoption, with no figures given. (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/411043887_Artificial_Intelligence_in_Diagnostic_Radiology_Current_Applications_Clinical_Impact_and_Future_Perspectives)
- **drug discovery and trials**: A peer-reviewed analysis from a limited sample (includes BCG researchers) reports about 80–90% Phase I and about 40% Phase II success for AI-discovered molecules, comparable to historic averages. (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S135964462400134X)
- **drug discovery and trials**: A secondary/vendor citation of Oliver Wyman June 2026 figures reports 118 AI-native Phase I programs since 2016, with 73% (38/52) advancing to Phase II and 41% (9/22) advancing from Phase II. (https://axis-intelligence.com/ai-drug-discovery-statistics)
- **drug discovery and trials**: A secondary/vendor news report states Insilico's rentosertib entered Phase III (announced July 7, 2026), with the first patient dosed on September 9, 2026. (https://allsci.com/news/clinical-trials/ai-drug-phase-iii-ipf-trial-insilico-advances/)
- **drug discovery and trials**: An unverified source reports about $60B invested in AI drug discovery since 2019, about 175 clinical-stage programs, and no AI-discovered drug approved yet. (https://intuitionlabs.ai/articles/ai-discovered-drugs-clinical-trials-2026)
- **administration and payers**: A secondary law-firm review (date inferred from URL) reports multiple US states passed 2026 laws on insurer AI in prior authorization and claims, covering transparency and human oversight of adverse determinations, with no count given. - **clinical documentation and workforce**: A peer-reviewed three-arm RCT of 238 outpatient physicians (Nov 4, 2024–Jan 3, 2025) reports Nabla cut documentation time and scribe use was linked to modest gains in burnout and task load versus usual care.
